Our Selling Programs

Six ways we can help you sell

We go far beyond the standard cash offer. Every situation is different — so we built solutions for all of them.

24-Hour Cash Out

Our fastest option. We make a direct cash offer and close on your schedule — no repairs, no showings, no waiting.

Seller Finance

You act as the bank. We make monthly payments directly to you over an agreed term — often at a better effective price than a straight cash sale.

Payment Takeover (Sub-To)

We take over your existing mortgage payments — you transfer the deed and walk away. A great option when traditional selling isn’t ideal.

Equity Protection Plan

If you’ve got real equity built up, we structure the deal so you keep as much of it as possible — even in a complicated situation.

Hybrid Sales Approach

We list your property on the open market and pursue off-market buyers at the same time — whichever route gets you the better deal wins.

Traditional Market Listing

Sometimes listing the normal way really is the better move. We can run that process too, with real marketing and our own network of buyers behind it.

Common Questions

Questions we hear most often

We want you to feel completely comfortable before making any decision.

Do I need to fix anything before selling?

Not a thing. We evaluate your home exactly as it stands — no cleaning, no repairs, no staging required. We handle everything after the sale.

How quickly can I receive a cash offer?

Most sellers receive a clear offer within 24 to 48 hours of our initial review. We don’t make you wait weeks the way a traditional listing would.

Can I choose my own closing date?

Yes. Tell us your timeline and we’ll build the closing around it, whether that’s two weeks out or two months.

Will there be any pressure to accept the offer?

None whatsoever. Take as much time as you need to review, ask questions, and discuss it with your family. The decision is always entirely yours.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No agent fees, no commissions, no hidden costs. The offer we present is the amount you walk away with at closing.

What types of homes do you buy?

Single-family homes, multi-family properties, condos, and more — across our 12+ state network. Condition, age, and location rarely rule a property out.
