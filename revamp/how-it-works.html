FAQ

Common questions about the process

How long does the whole process take?

From your first call to closing, most transactions take between 10 and 30 days. If you need more time, that is completely fine — we work on your schedule, not ours.

Do I need a lawyer or real estate agent?

You are always welcome to involve your own attorney, and we encourage it. However, you do not need an agent — we handle the process directly and explain everything clearly.

Is the cash offer really guaranteed?

Yes. Unlike traditional buyers who depend on bank financing, our offers are backed by our own capital. Once we agree on a price, it does not change at closing.

What if I change my mind after accepting?

We aim to be as flexible as possible. We want you to feel comfortable with your decision — not locked in to something that isn’t right for you.
